Item detail — square by square idea-256 · study docs/prds/item-detail-squares.md · 430 px

The item stops being one row with footnotes and becomes the SKU, everywhere it is: one block per row, the row's squares with this SKU lit, and its pallets drawn square by square. ⇄ opens the Move sheet and comes back. Numbers, letters, ? and the dashed pile are tapped to correct, in amber, with the Stock card's confirmation. Real SKUs from prod, 7 Oct 2026.

The visual language

  • Amber = a square (letters, lit squares), what waits to be saved (ring, bar), and the ? nobody has answered. The +n loose / −n extra chip is amber without a ring: a fact.
  • Pink 🪜 = a top. Green = the Move sheet and the flash of what just moved.
  • Red = a square over 30. Dashed = loose units. White edge = the row you tapped.
  • Correcting (numbers, letters, ?, +) is amber and writes an EDIT on Save. Moving is ⇄ and writes a MOVE. Two colours, two logs.

What leaves the item

  • WHERE as a way to move — it overwrote the destination's boxes when the SKU was already there (24 MOVEs, 512 units in 30 days). Moving is ⇄. ❓1
  • «Also» → other places — they are blocks now. «Also» keeps reservations (of every row) and the last moves.
  • ⋯ → Distribution — a third editor of boxes, without squares. Edit on the drawing.
