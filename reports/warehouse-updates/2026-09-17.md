# PickD — What's new

**September 17, 2026**

---

## What you will notice

### Cancelling a combined order now cancels the whole pallet.

Orders that were combined show up in Ship as one card: the order numbers across the top, and one
row of numbers underneath that adds up all of them. **Cancel Order on that card only cancelled one
of them** — the oldest — and the message it gave back was easy to read as normal: _"Order
cancelled — 5 units to RETURN TO STOCK"_, under a card that said 8 units.

The rest of the card stayed exactly as it was: finished, waiting to ship, with its bikes still
counted as picked. On two cards today that came to **8 bikes of JAX OUTDOOR GEAR** that PickD had
already taken off the shelves and that no longer belonged to any order — 3 FAULTLINE A3 V2 17 from
ROW 34, 2 FAULTLINE A3 V2 19 from ROW 38, a HELIX 16 from ROW 2, a Helix 18 from ROW 8, and a
RENEGADE A1 LTD 51 from ROW 25. Stock that is not on the shelf sends the next picker to an empty
spot, and an order that reads as finished when nobody is going to ship it is one more thing to
explain at the end of the week.

Fixing it by hand did not work either. Once the first order of a combined one had been cancelled,
cancelling the second came back red — _"Failed to cancel order: tuple to be updated was already
modified…"_ — and nothing was returned.

Now the card is the pallet. The question is **"Mark all 3 orders of this combined order as
cancelled?"**, and the message afterwards is **"3 orders cancelled — 8 units to RETURN TO
STOCK"** — the same number the card was showing. If any of those orders is marked as shipped it
still asks first, the same **Never shipped?** question as before, and nothing is written until you
answer it.

### An order that is already finished no longer changes carrier by itself.

When one order left a combined group, the ones that stayed behind could switch from truck to
**FedEx** on their own — including orders that were already verified and waiting to ship. That is
how the pallet travels and what the station picks in Audit Source or in the FedEx system, so it was
a decision nobody made. Orders that are still open are reclassified as before.
