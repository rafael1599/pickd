## What you will notice

### Kids' bikes come off the pallet and get picked last.

A JUV Laser 1.6 ships in a box 37 inches long and 18 high. A Citizen 2 ships in one 55 by 29.
Double-Check counted them the same — twelve units to a pallet, whatever was inside them — so an
order of twenty-two Lasers was drawn as two pallets, one of twelve and one of ten. That is a
pallet nobody can actually build, and a pallet count that went into Audit Source as if those were
two pallets of grown bikes.

Now they behave like parts. They leave the pallet arithmetic and they show up at the end of the
order in their own group, **Small bikes**, so they are picked last.

**39 SKUs, 1,202 units in stock**: the whole juvenile line, plus the Taxi on 16, 20 and 24 inch
wheels. The 26 inch Taxi is a full bike and stays on the pallet. Of the 72 orders in the last four
months that carried one of these, **71 are now grouped differently** — order 881518 was drawn as
five pallets of twelve and is now three pallets plus 27 kids' bikes.

> ### ⚠️ This has not been proven on a real order yet
