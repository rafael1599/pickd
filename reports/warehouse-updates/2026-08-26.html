PickD — What changed today

Wednesday, August 26, 2026 · for everyone on the Ludlow floor

The short version

1. Orders no longer say LOW STOCK for a bike that is sitting on the shelf under its "other" name.

2. Registering a new SKU from Double Check now clears the red badge right away — and it never offers to register the same SKU twice.

3. Every SKU now has one way to be written: the AS400 way, with the zero (01-0530). You can keep typing it short — the app finds it and saves it right.

4. Double Check now handles a LOW STOCK line by itself: it swaps the bike when it can, and when it can't it tells you exactly what it found and gives you the buttons right there — no Edit Order needed.

What you will notice

1. "LOW STOCK" on a bike that is right there

BeforeSome bikes exist under two names in the system — for example 03-3768BL and 03-3768BLD are the same Riptide. When an order asked for one name and the stock was filed under the other, the order came in as LOW STOCK with no location, and someone had to swap it by hand. It happened again this morning on order 881288 with 145 bikes on ROW 43.
NowThe system checks both names and picks the one that actually has stock — when the order is created, and again when you open Edit Order. If a bike still shows LOW STOCK after this, it really is not on the shelf.

2. Registering a SKU from Double Check

BeforeLong-press → "Bike or Part" → save… and the card stayed red ("UNREG"). Long-pressing again offered to register it a second time, which created duplicates. Behind the curtain, 96 half-registered SKUs had piled up with nothing on any shelf.
NowThe moment you save, the card turns normal. If you saved it with 0 units, long-press shows that row with an Edit button — add the quantity there, don't register again. The 96 leftovers were cleaned up (the ones with real history were kept).

3. One way to write a SKU

AS400 always writes the number with four digits: 01 0288. Over time we ended up with three spellings of the same thing — 01-0288, 01-288, 010288 — and the same part could be counted under two of them. From today the system saves every SKU the AS400 way, no matter how it is typed:

4. LOW STOCK no longer waits for Edit Order

BeforeDouble Check showed the yellow LOW STOCK or red UNREG badge and nothing else. To find out why — and to do anything about it — you had to open Edit Order, where the automatic swap and the suggestions lived.
NowThe moment the order is on screen, Double Check checks the stock itself. If the same bike has enough units under its other name, it swaps it for you and says so (with an Undo). If not, a note under the card says exactly what it found, and the buttons for that one line are right there.

Mentioned only, so you know it happened

  • These changes are locked in place: the system itself now refuses a second spelling of a SKU, keeps the LOW STOCK / UNREG status up to date on its own, and won't create half-registered SKUs anymore.
  • The order scanner on the Bay 2 laptop needs its update installed (Rafael) — until then, new orders may still arrive with the old spelling, and Edit Order fixes them on open.
  • Every renamed SKU shows its old name in History as "Was: …", and there is a full list of what was renamed.

Coming up
