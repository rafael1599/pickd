PickD — What's new

Thursday, August 27, 2026 · everything since the August 26 update · for everyone on the Ludlow floor

The short version

1. Ship: adding a bike to a completed order no longer turns it into a "part" — and every line now shows its weight.

2. Ship: tap a SKU to open it, carriers show 3 + "…", photos show 2 + a menu, and the "Verified" pill is gone.

3. FedEx orders show the Recipient ID to paste into Ship Manager, in Ship and in Double Check.

What you will notice

1. Ship — a combined bike counts as a bike, and weights are on every line

BeforeYesterday order 881303 (one Riptide, registered since February) was combined into completed order 881301. Ship counted it as a part, asked for its weight, and saved "8 bikes + 1 part" with a total that made no sense.
NowShip reads each line's bike/part and weight from the order itself the moment it opens, so the count is right from the first second. The Order Items table has a new Lbs column: what each line weighs (unit × quantity). A ? in orange means no weight is on file yet.

2. Ship — four small things you asked for

WhereNow
Order ItemsTap a SKU to open its item detail. If the SKU sits in more than one place, you get the same list of locations as the long-press in Double Check, with Edit on each row.
Carrier pickerShows R+L, FedEx, RIST (the three most used) plus whichever is selected; … shows the rest.
Pallet photosTwo thumbnails at most; the +N tile opens Take photo / View all.
Order cardThe green Verified label is gone — every order in Ship is already verified. Waiting and Editing still show.

3. FedEx Recipient ID, right where you ship

The number the ship station types into FedEx Ship Manager is the customer's AS400 account plus the two-digit ship-to (0010495 00 → 1049500). PickD now keeps it and shows it as a chip on the Ship card and in Double Check, only on FedEx orders, with a copy button and the one instruction for Ship Manager. Orders captured before this get their number filled in by the new Maintenance button on the Bay 2 watcher (preview first, then apply).

4. On the Bay 2 watcher

  • Every card shows the Ship-to name next to the Bill-to — for a consumer-direct or warranty order the Bill-to is the channel, not the person.
  • New ⋯ → Maintenance panel: one card per action with a one-line explanation, Preview (nothing written) and Apply. First action: the AS400 account backfill.
  • A line the catalog does not know is now written the way the box says it (03-3768BL, not 03-3768BLD), so registering it makes the order match right away.

These need the watcher update installed on the Bay 2 Mac.

Mentioned only, so you know it happened

  • Stock moves no longer glue notes onto item names ("… | auto-restore on cancel") — the note goes to the item's note field; the three names that still carried one were cleaned.
  • The AS400 "D" spellings (03-3768BLD, 03-3769BLD, 03-3779RDD) were merged into the 2-letter names the boxes use. Stock unchanged.
  • Safeguards on the code side (tests run on every push) and a tidier backlog.
Still from the floor: the physical check of the merged parts (now 25 — the 22 from yesterday plus the three above), and a real order with LOW STOCK opened in Double Check to see the new panel in action.
