Bay 3 is being worked north–south. The east–west version holds more but buries the middle rows of a deep block, so it is parked — it is now the E–W ROWS toggle inside each zone page rather than a page of its own, which is what let the frozen copy on the old 56" × 54" pallet go.
Every zone page is drawn by the same engine off the same measurements, at the pallet size in the boxes at the top of the page. Change the size and the counts change with it. The combined schematic is the one exception, and says so on its card. Bay 2 south, Bay 1 north and the office gap are modelled too; the map opens them.
Scale 10 px = 1 ft. Structural posts are measured in Bay 3 North and Bay 2 and deducted; everywhere else areas are gross and real rackable space is somewhat less.
